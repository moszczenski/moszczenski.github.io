# BMLabs

BMLabs builds apps that solve real everyday problems, on phones, tablets and the web.
Every app starts from a real need, keeps the solution simple and respects your privacy.

## Current Apps

**Picture Charades**: charades for children who can't read yet. The app deals picture
cards, times each turn and keeps score, so the grown-ups can just play along.
No ads, no tracking, no data collected.
Coming soon to Google Play.
